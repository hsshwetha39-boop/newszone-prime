Prerna Wanvari Challenges 'First Indian Presenter' Claim About Vijay Sethupathi at International Emmy Awards

By Shwetha • November 9, 2026 • 3 min read

Actor Prerna Wanvari has challenged reports claiming that Tamil actor Vijay Sethupathi will become the first Indian to present an award at the International Emmy Awards. She has pointed out that several Indian television personalities had already appeared as presenters at the international ceremony years before his scheduled participation.

Vijay Sethupathi is reportedly set to be one of the presenters at the 54th International Emmy Awards, which will take place in New York on November 23, 2026. The announcement generated excitement among his fans, with several reports describing his selection as a historic first for India.

However, Prerna Wanvari questioned the accuracy of that description through a post on her Instagram Stories. Reacting to a report referring to Vijay as the first Indian presenter, she wrote, "You got this so wrong! And before me were two iconic women @aashkagoradia and @realsimonesingh who were first to present at The International Emmys in 2009 and 2010."

Wanvari also shared a photograph of herself presenting an award at the International Emmy Awards in 2012, drawing attention to the earlier participation of Indian television personalities.

Prerna Wanvari Recalls Earlier Indian Presenters

According to Wanvari, Aashka Goradia and Simone Singh were the Indian presenters at the International Emmy Awards in 2009 and 2010 respectively. Wanvari herself presented the Arts Programming award at the 40th International Emmy Awards in 2012.

She clarified that Vijay Sethupathi's participation at the upcoming ceremony is certainly worth celebrating, but said that calling him the first Indian presenter overlooked the contributions of those who had represented India before him.

Her remarks have brought renewed attention to the earlier presence of Indian television actresses at the global awards ceremony. She argued that these appearances should also receive recognition rather than being overlooked while celebrating a new announcement.

The discussion is not about questioning Vijay Sethupathi's selection as a presenter, but about ensuring that claims of a historic first accurately reflect the event's earlier history.

Some reports have also noted that Priyanka Chopra presented at the Primetime Emmy Awards in Los Angeles in 2016. That is a separate ceremony from the International Emmy Awards, which honour television made outside the United States.

Vijay Sethupathi to Present at the 54th International Emmy Awards

Vijay Sethupathi's participation at the upcoming ceremony was first reported by The Hollywood Reporter India. The actor is expected to represent Indian entertainment as one of the presenters at the November 23 event in New York.

The 54th International Emmy Awards will also feature Indian nominees in two categories. SonyLIV's series Real Kashmir Football Club has received a nomination in the Best Drama Series category, while comedian Vir Das's Netflix special Fool Volume is competing in the Best Comedy Series category.

Vir Das previously hosted the International Emmy Awards in 2024, becoming the first Indian to do so, after winning the award for his comedy special Vir Das: Landing in 2023.

The debate surrounding the presenter announcement has highlighted the importance of recognising earlier contributions by Indian television artists. While Vijay Sethupathi's upcoming appearance is attracting attention, Prerna Wanvari's intervention has reminded audiences that Indian performers have been part of the International Emmy stage for many years.

Sources: Prerna Wanvari's Instagram Story and reports by WION, Sacnilk, Outlook India and The Hollywood Reporter India.
