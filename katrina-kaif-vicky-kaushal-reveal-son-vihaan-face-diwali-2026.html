Katrina Kaif and Vicky Kaushal Reveal Son Vihaan's Face for the First Time on Diwali, Call It Their 'Brightest Diwali Yet'

By Shwetha • November 9, 2026 • 3 min read

Bollywood actors Katrina Kaif and Vicky Kaushal made Diwali 2026 especially memorable for their family and fans by sharing the first clear public photograph of their son, Vihaan Kaushal. The couple posted a family picture on social media on November 8, giving followers their first proper glimpse of the little boy's face since his birth.

Sharing the photograph, Katrina and Vicky described the occasion as their "brightest Diwali yet" and extended festive wishes to everyone. Their caption read, "Our brightest Diwali yet... Wishing everyone love, light and happiness. - Vicky, Katrina & Vihaan."

The photograph features the couple posing with their son in coordinated traditional outfits. Katrina is dressed in a bright yellow embroidered ethnic suit, while Vicky wears an elegant cream-coloured traditional ensemble. Vihaan complements his parents in a cream kurta-pyjama paired with a yellow jacket.

The toddler can be seen smiling and waving at the camera, making the family photograph a special moment for fans who had been waiting to see his face. Katrina and Vicky are seen sitting beside their son, sharing the festive moment together.

Face Reveal Comes a Day After Vihaan's First Birthday

The couple's Diwali post came just one day after Vihaan celebrated his first birthday on November 7, 2026. He was born on November 7, 2025, and the actors announced his arrival through a joint social media message expressing their happiness and gratitude.

At the time, they wrote, "Our bundle of joy has arrived. With immense love and gratitude, we welcome our baby boy."

In January 2026, the couple introduced their son by revealing his name, Vihaan Kaushal. They shared a photograph showing the baby's tiny hand held between their hands and described him as their ray of light. However, they had largely kept his face away from public view until the latest Diwali photograph.

The decision to share his first clear public photograph has attracted considerable attention, with fans celebrating the special family occasion.

Bollywood Celebrities React to the Family Photograph

Several film personalities and friends from the entertainment industry responded to the couple's post with affectionate messages and festive wishes.

Ananya Panday wrote "Oh my god !!!" in the comments, while Sonam Kapoor wished the family a happy Diwali. Kiara Advani dropped heart emojis, and Kareena Kapoor Khan affectionately called the little boy "Puddinggggg Baba". Karan Johar wished Vihaan a happy birthday, and other celebrities, including Arjun Kapoor, Mrunal Thakur, Malaika Arora and Huma Qureshi, also reacted to the post.

The photograph quickly became a talking point among fans, who welcomed the rare glimpse of the couple's son and sent birthday and Diwali wishes to the family.

Katrina Kaif and Vicky Kaushal's Journey as Parents

Katrina Kaif and Vicky Kaushal married on December 9, 2021, in a private wedding ceremony at Six Senses Fort Barwara in Rajasthan. Since then, the actors have generally maintained a private approach towards their personal lives.

Their son Vihaan's arrival in November 2025 marked a new chapter for the couple. Vicky has previously spoken about how fatherhood has changed his perspective, describing the experience as magical and explaining how watching his wife and child share happy moments has made him more sensitive and responsible.

The couple's latest Diwali post offered fans a rare glimpse into their family life. With Vihaan celebrating his first birthday just a day before the festival, the photograph marked a particularly memorable occasion for the Kaushal family.

Sources: the couple's Instagram post and reports by India TV, IANS, Outlook India, Free Press Journal and WION.
