PM Modi Visits LK Advani on 99th Birthday as Veteran BJP Leader Enters His 100th Year

By Shwetha • November 9, 2026 • 3 min read

Prime Minister Narendra Modi visited veteran Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leader and former Deputy Prime Minister Lal Krishna Advani at his residence on Sunday, November 8, to extend birthday wishes as the senior leader entered the 100th year of his life. Advani turned 99 on the occasion, marking another milestone in his decades-long political journey.

The visit was part of the birthday greetings extended to Advani by senior political leaders. Modi also shared a message on social media, describing November 8 as a landmark day and acknowledging the veteran leader's contribution to Indian politics and public life.

In his message, the Prime Minister recalled Advani's journey from experiencing the Partition of India and rebuilding his life to strengthening the Bharatiya Jana Sangh and helping establish the BJP as a major political force. He also highlighted Advani's role during the Emergency, his leadership of the Somnath-to-Ayodhya Rath Yatra and his service in the Union government.

Modi further referred to Advani's interests beyond politics, including his passion for reading, culture and cinema, as well as his work as an author. The Prime Minister also wrote an article reflecting on the veteran leader's life and contributions.

BJP Organises Puja and Havan at Party Headquarters

The BJP marked Advani's 99th birthday with a special puja and havan at its headquarters in New Delhi. The religious ceremony was held between 10 am and 10.30 am and was attended by senior party leaders, including BJP national president Nitin Nabin, and Advani's daughter, Pratibha Advani.

The programme was organised to recognise Advani's long association with the party and his contribution to its organisational development. A video presentation of about 20 minutes, highlighting important moments from his political career and excerpts from his speeches, was also part of the birthday event.

Nabin extended birthday wishes to Advani and described him as a source of inspiration for the party. He also acknowledged the veteran leader's role in the growth of the organisation and the Ram Janmabhoomi movement. Later in the day, Nabin also visited Advani's residence to personally convey his greetings, according to IANS.

LK Advani's Political Journey

Born on November 8, 1927, in Karachi, in present-day Pakistan, Lal Krishna Advani joined the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) in 1942. Following the Partition in 1947, he moved to India and went on to become one of the prominent figures in the Jana Sangh and the BJP.

Advani served as BJP president for three terms between 1986 and 2005. His political journey included a central role in the party's expansion and the Somnath-to-Ayodhya Rath Yatra in 1990, an important episode in the history of the Ram Janmabhoomi movement.

He later served as Union Home Minister and became India's Deputy Prime Minister from 2002 to 2004 during the government led by Atal Bihari Vajpayee. In recognition of his contribution to public life, he was conferred the Bharat Ratna in 2024.

As Advani entered his 100th year, birthday greetings from the Prime Minister and other political leaders brought renewed attention to his long career in Indian politics and his role in the development of the BJP.
