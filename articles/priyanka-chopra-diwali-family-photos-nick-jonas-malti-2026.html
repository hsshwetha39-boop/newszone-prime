Priyanka Chopra Shares Diwali Family Album With Nick Jonas and Daughter Malti From Los Angeles

By Shwetha • November 9, 2026 • 3 min read

Actor Priyanka Chopra Jonas gave fans a glimpse into her Diwali celebrations by sharing a collection of family photographs featuring her husband, American singer Nick Jonas, and their daughter, Malti Marie Chopra Jonas. The pictures, posted on November 8, 2026, captured the family's festive moments at their Los Angeles home.

Sharing the photographs on Instagram, Priyanka wrote, "Some camera roll favourites from this Diwali. Wishing you all a very Happy Diwali." The post featured a mix of family pictures, festive decorations and candid moments from the celebrations.

For the occasion, Priyanka chose a burgundy and gold traditional outfit featuring intricate embellishments, zardozi detailing, beadwork and gold-thread embroidery. She completed her look with statement gold earrings, matching bangles and a kundan maang tikka. Her hair was styled in a sleek low bun decorated with a white gajra.

Nick Jonas complemented his wife's festive appearance in a cream-and-white embroidered kurta set. Their daughter Malti also joined the celebrations in a white lehenga, coordinating with her father's outfit.

The photographs showed the family spending time together amid a festive setting decorated with candles, flowers and warm lights. One picture captured Priyanka and Nick with Malti, while another highlighted the detailed work on Priyanka's outfit.

Nick responded to his wife's post with festive emojis, including a diya and a star-eyed face, adding to the family's Diwali wishes.

Priyanka Continues to Celebrate Indian Festivals With Her Family

Although Priyanka lives in the United States, she regularly celebrates Indian festivals and shares glimpses of these occasions with her followers. Her recent festive posts have highlighted her efforts to introduce Malti to Indian customs and traditions while growing up abroad.

A few days earlier, Priyanka and Nick were also seen at a star-studded Diwali gathering in Los Angeles, according to reports.

During the family's Dussehra celebrations in October, Priyanka shared photographs from a series of religious rituals at their Los Angeles home. The celebrations included Shivling Abhishek, a hawan and Kanya Pujan. One photograph showed Nick participating in the Kanya Pujan ritual by washing Malti's feet.

Malti was dressed in an ivory outfit and wore a red scarf featuring the words "Jai Mata Di" during the Dussehra celebrations.

The family also marked Karwa Chauth together in Los Angeles. Priyanka previously revealed that Nick had arranged his tour schedule to ensure he could be home for the occasion. She expressed her appreciation for his efforts and shared photographs from their celebration.

Priyanka Chopra's Upcoming Film Projects

On the professional front, Priyanka is preparing for her appearance in filmmaker SS Rajamouli's upcoming film Varanasi. The project stars Mahesh Babu and Prithviraj Sukumaran alongside Priyanka and is scheduled to release in theatres on April 7, 2027.

She has also announced Ventilator 2 under her production banner, Purple Pebble Pictures. The upcoming film is a sequel to the 2016 Marathi movie Ventilator, which won three National Film Awards. Filmmaker Rajesh Mapuskar is returning as its writer and director, and the sequel is scheduled to release in 2027.

While Priyanka continues to work on international and Indian projects, her latest Diwali photographs offered followers a personal glimpse into her celebrations with Nick and Malti, highlighting the family's festive traditions and time spent together.

Sources: Priyanka Chopra's Instagram post and reports by IANS, WION, Free Press Journal, India.com, ANI and Outlook India.
