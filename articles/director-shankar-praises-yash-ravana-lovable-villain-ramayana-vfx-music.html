Director Shankar Praises Yash as a 'Lovable Villain' in Ramayana, Applauds Film's VFX and Music

By Shwetha • November 9, 2026 • 2 min read

Filmmaker S. Shankar, best known for Enthiran, 2.0 and Indian 2, has praised Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Rise of a Legend and singled out Yash's performance as Ravana. In a post on X on November 6, a day after the film opened in theatres, he called the film a spectacle and described Yash as a "lovable villain".

"#Ramayana – What a spectacle!!! Never seen visuals like this in Indian cinema," Shankar wrote, adding that the whole team deserved applause for making Indian cinema proud. About Yash, he said: "It's a delight to watch @TheNameIsYash in every frame. He is such a lovable villain and has delivered a quality performance."

He also sent his best wishes to Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi, and credited producer Namit Malhotra and director Nitesh Tiwari for their effort. According to reports, he singled out the film's visual effects, production values, look and feel, sound design and the music of A.R. Rahman and Hans Zimmer.

Tiwari replied to the post with folded-hands emojis and the words "Thanks a lot sir."

Why "Lovable Villain" Stands Out

The phrase drew attention because Ravana is traditionally the epic's main antagonist. Tiwari has said earlier that his Ramayana would not show Ravana as a one-dimensional villain, and that the character would be presented with many layers. Since the release, viewers have been divided on social media over whether the film glorifies the character, and Yash has said that "none of us are bigger than Ramayana".

Shankar's remark was a personal comment on Yash's screen presence and acting. It was not a statement about the mythological character himself.

How the Film Has Been Received

Filmmaker Sandeep Reddy Vanga has also shared a positive reaction to the film. Overall reviews have been mixed, and reports say that Ranbir Kapoor's performance as Lord Rama has drawn mixed responses online, while Yash's Ravana has received a lot of praise. Outlook India reported that the film earned ₹221.05 crore worldwide in its first two days.

About the Film

Ramayana: Rise of a Legend is directed by Nitesh Tiwari and produced by Namit Malhotra's Prime Focus Studios in association with DNEG and Yash's Monster Mind Creations. The first part was released worldwide on November 5, 2026, in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

Ranbir Kapoor plays Lord Rama, Sai Pallavi plays Sita and Yash plays Ravana. Ravie Dubey appears as Lakshman, Arun Govil as Dasharatha, Lara Dutta as Kaikeyi and Rakul Preet Singh as Surpanakha. An end-credit sequence teases Sunny Deol as Lord Hanuman and announces that Part 2, which is expected to bring Rama's battle against Ravana in Lanka, will release during Diwali 2028.

Sources: Shankar's post on X and reports by Outlook India, Free Press Journal, NewsBytes, IBC World News, India.com and India TV.
