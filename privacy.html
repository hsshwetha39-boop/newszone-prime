Privacy Policy

At NewsZone Prime, we respect your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We only receive personal information that you choose to send us, for example your name, email address and message when you use our contact form or email us. We use it only to reply to you.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which stories are popular). It collects usage data such as pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, using cookies. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use them for analytics, to remember your dark-mode preference, and, where enabled, to show advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as well.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party vendors. These vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

External links

Our stories may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
