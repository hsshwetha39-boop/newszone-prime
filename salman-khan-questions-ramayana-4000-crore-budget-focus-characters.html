Salman Khan Questions ₹4,000 Crore Ramayana Budget Talk, Says Focus Should Be on Ram, Sita and Ravana

By Shwetha • November 8, 2026 • 3 min read

Bollywood actor Salman Khan has shared his views on the widespread discussion surrounding the reported ₹4,000 crore budget of Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana. The actor believes that conversations about the ambitious adaptation should focus on the epic's story, characters and cultural significance rather than being dominated by its production costs and visual effects.

Speaking at the trailer launch of his upcoming film Maatrubhumi: May War Rest In Peace in Mumbai on November 6, Salman questioned the repeated attention given to the film's budget and VFX. He said that Ramayana deserves to be discussed for what it represents, particularly its central characters and the scriptures on which the epic is based.

"I just think Ramayana should be discussed for Ramayana and not its VFX or its cost of production. I think this narrative that Rs 4,000 crore... VFX, VFX, I just don't think that is correct. Ramayana should be discussed for its characters, Ram, Ravana, Sita, on the scriptures," Salman said.

The actor also spoke about the qualities associated with Lord Ram and drew a comparison with the character he portrays in Maatrubhumi, Colonel B. Santosh Babu. He described Ram as an ideal husband, son, father, brother and warrior, adding that his character in the upcoming film possesses similar qualities.

Salman was also asked whether he had watched the new adaptation. In response, he mentioned that he had seen Ramanand Sagar's popular television version of Ramayana.

Salman Recalls Shelved Ramayana Film With His Brother

During the interaction, Salman revisited an interesting episode from his own career. He revealed that around 20 years ago, he and his brother, Sohail Khan, had planned to make a film based on Ramayana, with Salman expected to play Lord Ram.

Recalling the incident, the actor said that after the project was announced, photographers captured him and his brother leaving a location late at night. He said the media subsequently portrayed them as Ram and Lakshman by placing crowns on their photographs.

Salman recalled that the coverage became a talking point, and the project was eventually shelved. His comments offered a glimpse into an earlier attempt to bring the epic to the big screen that did not move forward.

Media reports over the years have also described an earlier Ramayana project from the 1990s, directed by Sohail Khan, with Salman reportedly set to play Ram. According to reports, the exact reasons that film was shelved have never been officially confirmed, so the account above is Salman's own recollection.

Ramayana's Reported Budget and Cast

Directed by Nitesh Tiwari, Ramayana features Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, Sai Pallavi as Sita and Yash as Ravana, with Sunny Deol also part of the cast. The first part, titled Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, was released on November 5, 2026, and reportedly crossed ₹100 crore in India within two days, according to figures from Sacnilk cited by media.

The reported ₹4,000 crore figure has become a major talking point around the project. Reports describe it as the combined budget of the two-part saga, rather than the cost of the first instalment alone.

Salman Khan's Maatrubhumi Release Date

Salman's remarks came during the trailer launch of Maatrubhumi: May War Rest In Peace. The film is now set to release on December 4, 2026, after a delay.

With his comments, Salman has urged audiences to look beyond the production scale of Ramayana and pay greater attention to the characters, values and story at the heart of the epic.

Sources: reports by Free Press Journal, WION, Outlook India, Pinkvilla, Glamsham and India.com.
