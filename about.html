About NewsZone Prime

NewsZone Prime is an independent news blog run by Shwetha. We cover film, entertainment, politics, sports, and the stories that are trending across India, in simple language that is quick to read.

What we cover

Our main sections are Film, Entertainment, Politics, Sports, Trending and Viral stories, plus important national news from India.

How we work

Our stories are written by us in our own words, based on publicly available reports and official statements. When something is not officially confirmed, we say so using words such as "reportedly" or "according to reports", and we try to update stories when new facts come in.

Corrections

If you see a mistake or want to report an issue with any story, please write to us at hsshwetha13@gmail.com. We review every message and fix verified errors quickly.
