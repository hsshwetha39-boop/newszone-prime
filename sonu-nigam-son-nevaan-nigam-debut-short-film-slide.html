Sonu Nigam Introduces 19-Year-Old Son Nevaan Nigam With Debut Short Film 'Slide'

By Shwetha • November 8, 2026 • 2 min read

Singer Sonu Nigam has introduced his 19-year-old son, Nevaan Nigam, to audiences through Slide, a science-fiction short film that marks the young artist's first project of his own in music and filmmaking. The project was unveiled around Diwali, with Sonu and his wife Madhurima Nigam sharing the announcement through a joint social media post.

In his message, Sonu reflected on his son's journey from a shy child to a young performer who has been working consistently to develop his musical and artistic abilities. The singer revealed that Nevaan was once hesitant to sing even in front of his own father, despite growing up in a family with a strong musical background.

Sonu also spoke about the discipline Nevaan has developed over the years. According to the singer, his son practises vocal exercises taught by his coach for up to 16 hours a day and has completed one-take studio recordings with renowned music producer Poo Bear. Nevaan has also been spending hours practising dance to improve his performance skills.

Recalling his son's early connection with music, Sonu mentioned that Nevaan recorded Kolaveri Di in a studio when he was just four years old. At around four and a half, he also performed his father's song Abhi Mujh Mein Kahin live on stage. Sonu acknowledged the affection audiences had shown Nevaan since those early appearances and requested their blessings as he begins a new chapter.

The singer emphasised that Nevaan intends to explore his own creative direction rather than simply follow the musical path established by his father and grandfather. He wished his son a journey guided by dignity, hard work, passion and humility.

Nevaan has also been in the public eye in recent years. In February 2025, he made his Instagram debut with a series of photographs showing his two-year physical transformation, and Sonu shared the post with a message of blessings.

About Nevaan Nigam's Short Film Slide

Slide combines music with a science-fiction storyline centred on a scientist who becomes stranded across different periods, ranging from the Wild West to the Disco Era. His journey takes an unexpected turn after he loses a special totem watch that works with his glove to operate a time-travel machine.

As he navigates unfamiliar eras and unexpected situations, the scientist encounters a girl and takes her on an extraordinary journey through time. The song Slide accompanies their adventure as he introduces her to different wonders of the world.

The short film has been directed by Alexander Cobian and Nevaan Nigam. Nevaan developed the concept, while the music was composed by The Audibles. The project serves as an introduction to his creative vision and his plans to explore music and visual storytelling.

With Slide, Nevaan begins his journey in the entertainment world, carrying the influence of his family's musical legacy while attempting to establish an identity of his own.

Sources: Sonu Nigam's Instagram post and a report by IANS.
